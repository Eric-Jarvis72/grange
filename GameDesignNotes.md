## Peaceful Farm Game
	• Clash of clans style layout/land expansion
	• Ever expanding
	• Hub world - Definitely has a casino.
		○ This is where you buy and sell seeds etc.
	• Subway/train station to visit other people's farms
		○ Can collaborate on farms via a whitelist system
	• Grangecoin is the currency
	• Always buy seeds
	• Everyone starts with a train, and a little run down farm
		○ You can buy more land, train carts, upgrades etc.
		○ Train upgrades, fertilizer, etc.
